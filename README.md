# Staff Support

Official website and documentation for Staff Support, the staff management bot for Study Basics.

## About

Staff Support is a Discord bot designed to assist the Study Basics staff team with moderation, server management, and other server-related tasks.

## Usage

Staff Support is intended exclusively for use within the Study Basics Discord server.

The bot and its services may not be used for personal purposes or outside of Study Basics.
