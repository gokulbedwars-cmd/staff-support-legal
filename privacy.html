Privacy Policy

Privacy information for Staff Support, the official Study Basics Discord bot.

1. Information We Handle

Staff Support may process information that Discord makes available to the bot when commands or server features are used. This can include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, roles, and command-related information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate, maintain, secure, and improve Staff Support and its features within Study Basics.

3. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell or share user information for advertising purposes. Information may be disclosed when required to address security, abuse, or legal obligations.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the bot's operation, moderation, security, or other legitimate server purposes.

5. Contact

For questions about privacy or data handled by Staff Support, contact the Study Basics administration team through Discord.
